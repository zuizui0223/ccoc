# Cover letter template — Theoretical Ecology

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Causal Compression under Open Composition,”** for publication as a regular article in *Theoretical Ecology*.

The paper asks a theoretical-ecology question about state choice under changing future interactions. We hold a finite controlled system fixed and vary only the declared legal future grammar. The main result shows that an exact response interface can remain extremely small under the closed grammar yet become maximally large after a minimal opening of legal future behavior. An explicit family changes from two exact response classes to a discrete \(2^{m+1}\)-state open quotient, while retaining a fixed four-symbol action alphabet, bounded local state and message alphabets, pairwise radius-one dynamics, maximum degree three, and logarithmic causal access. A complementary sufficient result identifies when a coarse macro-law remains portable under future expansion.

We have deliberately separated this contribution from classical contextual minimization, generic quotient refinement, state-reduction noncommutation, and modular sequential-machine compilation. The ecological contribution is the quantitative demonstration that future addressability can itself force state information even when local interaction structure remains simple.

The manuscript is theorem-first and non-empirical. It does not claim that the synthetic relay construction is a literal ecosystem or that the relevant ecological future grammar can be inferred from occurrence/association data alone.
