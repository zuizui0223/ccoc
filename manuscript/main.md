# Causal Compression under Open Composition

> Working manuscript scaffold. Target lane: Theoretical Ecology.

## Abstract

Ecological models routinely compress many micro-configurations into the same coarse state under a fixed set of future possibilities. We study when such compression remains valid after the legal future grammar is opened. For finite deterministic controlled systems, exact response equivalence is defined relative to a declared grammar of legal future actions. We show that small exact interfaces under every closed context do not imply a comparably small exact interface under an opened grammar, and we exhibit an explicit family in which each closed response interface has two states while the open interface has exponentially many states. The same family realizes this gap with fixed primitive controls, bounded local state, pairwise radius-one dynamics, and maximum degree three. We also state a conservative sufficient condition under which a finite macro-law remains portable as legal actions expand. The ecological interpretation is that present functional equivalence need not imply open-future causal equivalence: distinctions that are dormant under all currently admissible contexts may become necessary once colonization, reconnection, rewiring, or other future interactions are allowed. The results are theorem-first and contract-relative; they do not infer the relevant future grammar from empirical data.
