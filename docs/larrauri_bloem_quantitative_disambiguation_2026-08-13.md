# Larrauri–Bloem quantitative disambiguation — 2026-08-13

> **Purpose.** Prevent a misleading reading of the closest modern exact tail-minimization paper during the CCOC Tier-A novelty audit.

## Source checked

Alberto Larrauri & Roderick Bloem (2021), *Minimization and Synthesis of the Tail in Sequential Compositions of Mealy Machines*, arXiv:2105.10292v2.

The paper is directly relevant because it formalizes exact minimization of a tail Mealy machine under the input language generated by a known head and explicitly places the problem in the Kim–Newborn input-restriction lineage.

## The word “exponential” refers to two different resources

The abstract says that the new tail-minimization method gives an “exponential improvement over the state of the art.” In the body, this is explained as an **algorithmic representation/time improvement**, not as a theorem that context restriction produces an exponential ratio between two minimal exact machines.

The Kim–Newborn exact minimization route first determinizes the image automaton of the head. The resulting incompletely specified machine has size proportional to `2^|H| |T|`; applying an exponential-time IS-machine minimizer to that object yields a doubly-exponential procedure. Larrauri–Bloem avoid that determinization with observation machines and obtain a singly-exponential procedure.

Thus this exponential improvement concerns the **cost of computing the minimum replacement**, not the magnitude of a restricted-to-open minimum-state gap.

## Where an exponential minimum-size lower bound actually appears

The same paper does prove an exponential **solution-size** lower bound, but for the distinct **Tail Synthesis Problem**. There, the head `H` and desired whole-system model `M` are given and one must construct a missing tail `T`; Theorem 8 gives families for which every such tail has size exponential in the sizes of `H` and `M`.

That is a strong adjacent result, but it is not the CCOC comparison:

- CCOC keeps one deterministic plant/state space fixed;
- CCOC changes the legal future grammar used to define response equivalence;
- CCOC compares the exact quotient of that same plant under closed versus open grammars;
- the current extremal witness keeps every fixed closed quotient and their union at two classes while one newly legal primitive action makes the open quotient discrete.

Tail synthesis instead asks for the size of a newly constructed missing component satisfying a composition equation.

## Claim-control consequence

Do not cite Larrauri–Bloem’s “exponential improvement” as prior evidence of an exponential **state-compression gap** between restricted and unrestricted grammars. It is evidence that exact context-restricted minimization is mature theory and that Kim–Newborn’s classical exact construction can have exponential intermediate representation size.

Also do not ignore the paper’s exponential synthesis lower bound: it shows that exponential component-size phenomena are already present in a very close compositional Mealy-machine setting. The remaining CCOC falsification target must therefore stay narrow:

> a same-plant, nested-legal-grammar theorem in which restricted exact quotients are `O(1)` but a tiny grammar relaxation forces `2^{Omega(m)}` response classes, ideally with a one-symbol/action relaxation.

No such theorem was identified in the reviewed Larrauri–Bloem minimization results.
