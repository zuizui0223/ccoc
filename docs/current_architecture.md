## 2. Retained quantitative strengthening

### Constrained codebooks

`addressable_codebooks.py` and `codebook_families.py` weaken the full-product premise: jointly realizable states may obey parity, fixed-richness, or other constraints while open addressability still forces a large exact interface. `docs/composition_code_rate.md` records the correlated-composition interpretation.

### Approximate addressability

`approximate_addressability.py` changes the premise from exact coordinate recovery to bounded decoding error. It is retained as a stronger-model extension, while the Fano/information machinery itself remains explicitly classical substrate.
