## 2. What “recovered” means

A row belongs in this record if CCOC/RACH actually posed a materially distinct scientific or evidence-validity question. Its present status may be:

- proved;
- sharpness/construction;
- no-go proved;
- refuted;
- corrected;
- sufficient/conditional;
- unresolved;
- deferred/not pursued;
- abandoned by program pivot;
- unmerged experimental;
- historical literature gate.

Recovery is **not** equivalent to endorsement and is **not** equivalent to current priority.
