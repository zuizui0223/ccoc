## First-paper claim

The paper asks one question:

> How large can the exact response-interface penalty become when one keeps the controlled plant fixed and enlarges only the legal future grammar?

Safe mathematical hierarchy:

- CORE-1: fixed-grammar exact response quotient is substrate, not novelty;
- CORE-2: operational/codebook separation is the formal cross-grammar lower-bound language;
- CORE-3: the strongest residual contribution candidate is the simultaneous extremal bounded-local realization;
- CORE-4/5: constructive and local obstruction boundaries.

The firstness-bearing slogan must not be “open systems destroy compression.” Context-dependent state minimization, restricted-input minimization, interacting-FSM minimization, partition refinement, and generic noncommutation have classical antecedents.
