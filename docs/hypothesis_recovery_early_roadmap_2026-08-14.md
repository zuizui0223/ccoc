## Why this is a hypothesis rather than project plumbing

The roadmap was testing a substantive methodological proposition: that a structural invariant/panel procedure remains reliable across different causal generator families rather than only inside a hand-written finite grammar. The proposed backend pair was explicitly meant to challenge mechanism recovery under different causal pathways.

It was not completed in that original form. Therefore it should not be rewritten as an achieved empirical/generative validation claim merely because later exact finite benchmarks are strong.
