## Current first-paper hypothesis closure

The publication spine is now the following recovered-and-adjudicated subset.

### Foundation

- `HYP-B06` / CORE-1: the legal-word response quotient is the coarsest exact deterministic interface for a declared finite controlled system and grammar.

### Headline negative result

- `HYP-C01`: small exact closed interfaces can coexist with a much larger exact open interface — proved.
- `HYP-C03` / CORE-2: operational addressability plus supplied closed factorizations yields the cross-grammar lower bound — proved.
- `HYP-C08`: full product independence is unnecessary; a sufficiently large jointly realizable addressable codebook suffices — corrected/generalized and proved.

### Extremal sharpness

- `HYP-C11`: one newly legal primitive action can create linear open-only innovation while the closed quotient remains tiny — proved.
- `HYP-C12`: the explicit family saturates the finite-domain innovation capacity — proved.
- `HYP-E01` / CORE-3: for every `m>=1`, the same extremal gap is realized with a fixed four-symbol primitive alphabet, degree at most three, bounded local alphabets, cut one, and logarithmic access — proved as an explicit family/sharpness construction.

### Positive and local boundaries

- `HYP-C05`: stagewise small interfaces do **not** imply one portable macro-law — refuted as a sufficient condition.
- `HYP-C06` / CORE-4: common macro dynamics plus coherent embeddings / uniform newly legal actions give sufficient portability criteria — proved, not claimed necessary.
- `HYP-C07` / CORE-5: a newly legal future that splits one proposed macro fiber invalidates that merge — local obstruction proved; the stronger global claim is refuted.

- `HYP-C13`: bounded graph degree alone does not imply the latency lower bound; bounded local information/update radius is additionally required.
- `HYP-C14`: small physical cut width/treewidth does not upper-bound exact open response memory.
- `HYP-E03`: arbitrary multi-state grammar completion need not monotonically refine the canonical quotient.
- `HYP-E05`: arbitrary same-domain closed/open canonical quotients need not be nested.

## Final scientific conclusion supported by the recovered record

The present CCOC conclusion is narrower than many hypotheses explored during development:

> Exact compression is relative to a declared legal future. A state representation that is exact and extremely small under a closed future grammar need not remain comparably small when the future grammar is opened. If dormant distinctions become operationally addressable, the open exact response interface must retain them. This gap can be maximal on the declared finite comparison domain even when the underlying controlled network has a fixed primitive action alphabet, bounded local state/message alphabets, pairwise radius-one dynamics, maximum degree three, a one-edge focal/exterior cut, and logarithmic access. Conversely, an old macro-law can remain portable when newly legal behavior continues to descend uniformly through its macro fibers.

For the fixed-regular family,

\[
|P_C|=2,\qquad |P_O|=2^{m+1},\qquad K_O-K_C=m.
\]

This conclusion does **not** assert that contextual minimization, quotient refinement, generic noncommutation, pair separation, or bounded-local compilation are historically new. Those novelty claims were explicitly surrendered during the row-level prior-art audit.
