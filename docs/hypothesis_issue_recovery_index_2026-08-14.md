## Audit consequence

The issue history now has an explicit path into the hypothesis record. A closed issue is not allowed to disappear merely because its implementation was later renamed or its result was absorbed into a larger theorem family.

Remaining recovery work is therefore concentrated in **docs/modules/unmerged branches**, not in unmapped theorem-candidate issues.
