# Next-math candidate triage — 2026-08-13

> **Purpose.** Prevent CCOC from reopening nearby theorem branches that do not clear the current novelty bar. This is research triage, not a theorem.

## Decision

Do **not** open a new theorem branch merely because the H1–H4 historical compiler gate is temporarily waiting for primary copies. Two plausible extensions were checked and are not strong enough as the next CCOC novelty claim.

## 1. Small physical separator => small causal blanket — rejected

A purely static graph-separator premise cannot imply a small exact dynamic causal blanket.

The existing relay already provides the counterexample:

- the focal node `ROOT` connects to the entire exterior tree through one edge `ROOT -- body_root` for every `m`;
- local node/message alphabets remain bounded and maximum degree remains three;
- nevertheless, for `m` binary addressable exterior memories, the existing dynamic-blanket obstruction forces any exact exterior summary used with the focal bit to satisfy

\[
\log_2|B|\ge m.
\]

Therefore constant physical cut width can coexist with unbounded exact causal-boundary memory.

This is useful ecological interpretation and is recorded separately in `narrow_physical_boundary_not_causal_closure_2026-08-13.md`, but it is not a reason to create another theorem-registry branch. A narrow channel can time-multiplex many counterfactual exterior distinctions.

### Consequence for a future positive ecological theorem

A valid structural upper bound must assume more than topology. It must provide a biologically interpretable restriction that actually prevents exterior information from accumulating across alternative future interactions—for example a proven finite sufficient statistic or another dynamic factorization property. Repackaging graph cut size as a blanket is invalid.

## 2. Generic contraction / small-gain => epsilon blanket — not selected as CCOC novelty

A second possible branch was to assume contractive local dynamics and derive an approximate finite-radius interface: distant exterior perturbations decay, so truncating the exterior beyond a radius can bound response error.

This is mathematically sensible, but the generic idea sits directly inside mature approximate/compositional abstraction theory for interconnected control systems.

Primary preprints checked include:

- Rungger & Zamani (2015), *Compositional Construction of Approximate Abstractions of Interconnected Control Systems*, arXiv:1507.05014 — simulation functions and small-gain conditions for compositional approximate abstractions;
- Mallik et al. (2016), *Compositional Abstraction-Based Controller Synthesis for Continuous-Time Systems*, arXiv:1612.08515 — disturbance bisimulation and composition of input-to-state-stable nonlinear systems;
- Mallik et al. (2017), *Compositional Construction of Finite State Abstractions for Stochastic Control Systems*, arXiv:1709.09546 — stochastic incremental stability and compositional finite abstractions;
- Sadek et al. (2022), *Compositional Approximately Bisimilar Abstractions of Interconnected Systems*, arXiv:2211.08655 — approximate bisimulation under incremental input-to-state stability.

Accordingly, a theorem of the form

> local contraction / small gain implies an approximate finite abstraction or bounded output error

is not a defensible CCOC firstness target by itself.

This does **not** make approximate/stochastic portability a non-priority. The open problem remains to prove something specifically about **portability under grammar/composition expansion** that is not reducible to existing approximate bisimulation/simulation-function theory.

## 3. What still clears the novelty bar

A new CCOC theorem branch should proceed only if it supplies at least one of:

1. a genuinely nontrivial necessity/converse characterization for a delimited cross-grammar class;
2. a coupled memory/control/locality/latency lower bound not obtained by simply stacking coding and causal-cone inequalities;
3. an approximate/stochastic **portability-under-expansion** theorem whose content is not standard compositional approximate abstraction;
4. an ecological structural theorem that derives addressability or a finite dynamic blanket from explicit colonization/dispersal/interaction constraints, rather than assuming the dynamic factorization to be proved.

Until such a statement is identified, the correct work is the H1–H4 primary-source gate and manuscript transfer, not theorem count.
