# RACH asset map: active theorem core, future gold, and frozen support

This is a routing map, not a deletion list. The repository accumulated several
substantial implementations before its current theorem spine became clear. The
correct response is neither to pretend they are one theory nor to discard them
blindly. Retain each asset for the job it can actually do.

Read [the theorem spine](theorem_spine.md) first. This map answers the practical
question: **where should the next unit of work go?**

## Classification rule

An asset is **active core** only if it states or verifies one of the promotion
obligations in the theorem spine. An asset is **gold** if it can sharpen,
falsify, or empirically connect a future core theorem without changing that
theorem's subject. An asset is **frozen infrastructure** when it preserves proof
identity or history without strengthening a causal claim.

\[

candidate-mechanism obligations have been certified at the claimed level.}
\]

## 2. Gold seam A: joint open-and-candidate theorem machinery

The highest-value next theorem is not another toy witness. It should combine
external completion and candidate response type without silently assuming their
independence.

| Existing asset | Gold that remains | What it must not do |
|---|---|---|
| `addressable_completion_bounds.py` | template for explicit separating-word injections | do not assume exterior bits and response types automatically add |
| `dynamic_boundary_blankets.py` | positive finite-summary factorization language | do not call candidate-specific blankets universal without map agreement |
| `candidate_safe_laws.py` | response-type and set-valued law machinery | do not infer \(|Q|R\) without uniform response separation |
| `delayed_addressability.py` | grammar-state and delayed legal-word infrastructure | do not confuse delay with extra exterior memory |
| `relay_tree_compilation.py` | structural realization of selected-port witnesses | do not claim autonomous local-clock compilation of all grammar gates |

### Extraction target

Build a **joint open-and-candidate interface theorem** with an explicit common
state and explicit assumptions. Its output must distinguish:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\text{common dynamic blanket + common maps}
&\Rightarrow&
\text{universal deterministic open law},\\[4pt]
\text{common dynamic blanket + distinct response types}
&\Rightarrow&
\text{candidate-safe or set-valued open law},\\[4pt]
\text{no uniformly bounded blanket}
&\Rightarrow&
\text{open-memory obstruction}.
\end{array}
\]

A product lower bound may be added only after a concrete joint-separation
premise supplies separating words for every combined state distinction.

### Extraction target

Later, and only behind the exact structural interfaces now defined, extract:

\[
\text{data or solver output}
\to
C_t\text{ of retained completion/mechanism candidates}
\to
\text{universal, candidate-safe, set-valued, or UNRESOLVED verdict}.
\]

The adapter must accept arbitrary candidate-specific boundary and response
certificates. It must not assume floral mechanisms, Boolean motifs, or a fixed
small action vocabulary.

**Status:** preserve these modules and PR #31 as evidence-gateway staging. Do
not promote or expand them until the joint theorem tells them exactly what object
the evidence must retain.

## 4. Gold seam C: counterexample miner and exact proof kernel

| Existing asset | Gold that remains | Future use |
|---|---|---|
| `linear_proof_verifier.py` | exact rational witness and Farkas-certificate checking | verify finite/polyhedral counterexamples to proposed blanket or universality claims |
| `rational_polyhedral_inclusion.py` | exact inclusion checks | test a candidate summary against all declared completion responses |
| `polyhedral_motif_compiler.py` | compiler from restricted semantics to checkable finite queries | reuse only when a new theorem truly fits its grammar |
| replayable exact-proof modules | content-addressed proof objects | attach a counterexample or finite exhaustion to a theorem |
| finite theorem workflows | deterministic certificate replay pattern | make every new witness falsifiable and reproducible |

### Extraction target

Build a small **joint-counterexample protocol**:

```text
proposed common blanket / common macro-law condition
    -> bounded grammar and candidate-family search
    -> equal observed prefix with a separating future word or transition
    -> independently checked certificate
    -> prove, weaken, or reject the proposed condition
```

This machinery should mine and certify counterexamples. It should not dictate
what the theorem is about.

## 5. Gold seam D: adversarial model laboratory

| Existing asset | Gold that remains | Correct role now |

| `ecological_program.py` | grammar for conjunction, inhibition, alternatives, feasibility restrictions | hostile source of completion semantics, not the core ontology |
| `failure_modes.py` | counterexamples to hidden compatibility, inhibition, latent routes, noisy NULL interpretation | red-team every blanket or universality premise |
| `generative_benchmarks.py` | exact finite sweeps rather than Monte Carlo rhetoric | phase diagrams for restricted-theorem failure |
| `observation_envelope.py` | channel-induced decision envelopes | test which completions or response types an observation channel cannot distinguish |
| `observation_design.py`, `robust_panel_design.py`, `panel_phase_benchmarks.py` | discrete panel-selection machinery | later corollary: which interventions collapse a completion/candidate family |
| `benchmarks.py` | comparison to known finite truth | regression and calibration harness |

### Extraction target

Turn this layer into a **theorem red-team library**. It should test new claims
against small hostile grammars with latent completions, compatibility restrictions,
inhibition, simultaneous collisions, hidden state, delayed legal events, and
candidate response disagreement.

## 6. Freeze shelf: provenance is not the scientific claim

The following family is technically substantial but should not drive current
research work:

- `certificate_manifest.py`, `tiered_certificate_manifest.py`, and canonical
  JSON variants;
- `admission_transcript.py`, compiled/native transcript variants, and replay
  registries;
- signed transcript checkpoints;
- coverage-contract and transcript adapters; and
- tiered artifact plumbing around those formats.

They answer:

\[
\text{Which artifact was used, and has its history changed?}
\]

They do **not** answer whether an exterior completion remains possible, whether
a dynamic blanket is sufficient, or whether one macro transition is universal.

**Status:** keep tests passing. Add no new provenance feature unless a published
theorem certificate genuinely requires an audit chain. Do not casually delete
this code: exact-proof assets may still depend on it.

## 7. Compatibility shelf

`causal_model/__init__.py` is a broad backwards-compatible export surface for
older theorem, benchmark, and design modules. It is intentionally not the
research entrance. New theorem work must use `causal_model.current_theory`.

## Immediate queue

1. **Joint open-and-candidate criterion.** Formalize common dynamic blankets,
   candidate-specific maps, and the exact conditions for universal, candidate-safe,
   or set-valued open laws.
2. **Joint separation lower bound.** Give a non-circular condition under which
   exterior-completion and response-type memory are additive.
3. **Grammar-aware dynamic blanket theorem.** Extend positive factorization to
   summaries that include finite regular grammar state.
4. **Joint counterexample miner.** Search small hostile completion/candidate
   families for a future separator or macro-transition obstruction.
5. **Evidence bridge only after 1–4.** Narrow/rebase retained-family and e-process
   work behind generic candidate-boundary interfaces.
6. **Freeze audit work.** No new manifest, transcript, checkpoint, or provenance
   variant without a concrete theorem-publication requirement.

## Anti-queue

Do **not** spend the next cycle on:

- another coordinate toy whose only novelty is a larger state table;
- a provenance wrapper around an unchanged theorem;
- a domain-specific floral or ecological model inside this repository;
- a generic statistical backend unconnected to retained completion/mechanism
  families; or
- presenting a finite witness as a universal empirical claim.
